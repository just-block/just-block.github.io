JustBlock Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Introduction

JustBlock is a browser extension that helps users stay productive by blocking distracting websites. JustBlock is operated by BD1 LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("we", "us", "our"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the JustBlock extension and website.

Information We Collect

  • Blocking settings: The list of websites you block and your extension settings are stored locally in your browser. We do not receive or store your browsing history or your block list.
  • Purchase information: When you buy JustBlock Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We receive your email address and payment confirmation details from Stripe. We never receive or store your full card details.
  • License information: When you activate a license key, we store the key, its activation status, and a random identifier for the activated installation so we can validate your license.
  • Website analytics: Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect information such as pages visited and approximate location.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your license key by email and validate your license
  • To provide customer support and communicate with you about your purchase
  • To improve our website and extension

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Google Analytics for website analytics

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized personnel.

Data Retention

We retain purchase and license information for as long as your license is active and as required for accounting and legal purposes. To request access to or deletion of your personal information, contact us at 7gorbachevm@gmail.com.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email or within the JustBlock extension.
